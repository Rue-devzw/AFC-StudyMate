# AFM SEAR StudyMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 21, 2026

AFM SEAR StudyMate ("StudyMate", "we", "our", or "us") is a mobile application prepared for Android and iPhone use to support Bible engagement, Sunday School, discipleship, study planning, and ministry learning activities connected with Apostolic Faith Mission Southern and Eastern Africa.

This Privacy Policy explains what information StudyMate handles, where that information is stored, when it may leave your device, and the choices available to you.

This policy is intended to reflect the current mobile release of StudyMate as it operates today.

## 2. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the current Android and iPhone release of StudyMate, including features such as:

- onboarding and profile setup
- lesson reading and study progress
- Bible reading, bookmarks, highlights, and notes
- journal and reflection entries
- reminder scheduling
- teacher tools, including class roster and attendance support
- optional Google Sign-In for backup
- optional Google Drive backup and restore
- user-initiated sharing

This policy does not automatically apply to future web, desktop, server, or cloud-sync releases unless those services are separately published and clearly linked.

## 3. How StudyMate works today

The current mobile release of StudyMate is designed as a **local-first** app.

That means:

- most user data is stored on the device
- the app does **not** use church-managed realtime cloud sync for ordinary study activity
- the app does **not** store user study data in church-managed Firestore or Firebase Storage databases
- Google account sign-in is only used when a user chooses Google Drive backup features
- Google Drive backup is optional, not mandatory

In normal use, many features work without creating an account or uploading your study data to our servers.

## 4. Information StudyMate may store on your device

Depending on how you use the app, StudyMate may store the following information locally on your device:

- your display name or profile name entered in the app
- class, track, translation, and onboarding preferences
- lesson progress and completion history
- journal entries and saved reflections
- Bible bookmarks, highlights, and verse notes
- memory verse progress
- reminder settings and scheduled study times
- teacher-entered class roster information
- teacher-entered attendance records
- appearance preferences such as themes, fonts, and backgrounds

This local data is primarily stored on the phone or tablet where you use StudyMate.

## 5. Teacher-entered and ministry-managed information

Some users, especially teachers or ministry workers, may enter information relating to classes, learners, or attendance.

This may include:

- class roster names or identifiers
- attendance records
- study or ministry notes entered by the teacher

If you enter information about other people, you are responsible for ensuring you have the authority and ministry basis to manage that information and that your use of the app is consistent with church policy, school policy, parental or guardian expectations, and applicable law.

Because some of this information may relate to children or ministry members, users should take extra care when using shared or unsecured devices.

## 6. Optional Google Sign-In and Google Drive backup

StudyMate offers optional backup and restore using the signed-in user's own Google account.

If you choose to use Google Drive backup:

- you may be asked to sign in with Google
- the app may receive your Google account identity needed for sign-in, such as your name, email address, and basic profile details made available by Google
- the backup file is stored in your own Google Drive **appDataFolder**
- the backup file is not intended to be stored in a church-managed or developer-managed database
- the backup is used to help you restore your StudyMate data on a supported device

The backup may include user data exported from the app database, which can include local study data such as:

- profile and preference data
- lesson progress
- Bible notes, bookmarks, and highlights
- journal entries
- teacher-entered class and attendance information
- reminder and customization settings

Google processes Google account and Google Drive information under Google's own terms and privacy policy. Users who choose Google Drive backup should review Google's policies separately.

## 7. Information we do not intentionally collect remotely in the current release

In the current mobile release, StudyMate does **not intentionally use** church-managed remote storage for ordinary study data, and does **not intentionally send** the following to our own servers during normal use:

- realtime lesson sync records
- church-managed cloud backup copies of user study data
- church-managed Firestore user records
- church-managed Firebase Storage user backup files

In the current release, your study data ordinarily remains local unless you choose Google Drive backup.

## 8. Analytics and push messaging

The current app codebase may include support for analytics or push messaging infrastructure, but these are not part of the normal local-first study experience unless specifically configured and enabled by the publisher in a release build.

At the time of this policy:

- analytics are not part of ordinary local-first use
- push messaging is not required for the core study experience
- reminder notifications are primarily local reminder features

If analytics, push messaging, or additional remote services are materially enabled in a future release, this policy should be updated to describe what data is collected, what provider is used, and how users can manage those choices.

## 9. Permissions and device access

StudyMate may request or use limited device capabilities so app features can function properly.

### Notification permission

StudyMate may ask for notification permission when a user enables reminders, such as:

- daily study reminders
- Sunday School reminders
- Discovery reminders

If reminders are not enabled, notification access should not be required for that purpose.

### Internet access

Internet access may be used for:

- opening websites and external links
- Google Sign-In
- Google Drive backup and restore
- optional cloud-connected features if enabled in a future release

### Other access

StudyMate is not designed around continuous access to highly sensitive device data such as:

- precise location
- contacts
- microphone recordings
- personal photo library access for the core study experience

## 10. Sharing and user-directed disclosure

StudyMate includes user-controlled sharing features.

When you choose to share verses, notes, lesson excerpts, or other app content, the selected information is handed to the destination app, platform, or service you choose.

Once content is shared to another app or service, that destination may process the information under its own privacy policy. We are not responsible for how third-party destination apps handle information after you share it.

## 11. Third-party services and software components

StudyMate uses third-party software libraries and platform services to provide app functionality. Depending on the feature being used, these may include:

- Flutter and mobile platform runtime components
- Android and iOS system services
- device text-to-speech services
- Google Sign-In
- Google Drive API for optional user-owned backups
- optional Firebase-related infrastructure present in the codebase for selected release configurations
- open source and commercial libraries used for storage, rendering, document viewing, and app functionality

Third-party license notices are available from the app's licenses screen in Settings.

The presence of a library or framework in the codebase does not necessarily mean that user data is actively being sent to that service in the current release.

## 12. How we use information

StudyMate uses information primarily to:

- personalize the app experience
- remember your study preferences
- save your notes, bookmarks, and journal entries
- support lesson progress and study continuity
- provide teacher tools such as roster and attendance support
- schedule reminders you choose to enable
- allow optional backup and restore through your own Google Drive account
- support user-initiated sharing

We do not describe StudyMate as a general-purpose advertising or commercial profiling app.

## 13. Data retention

In the current local-first mobile release:

- local app data remains on your device until you delete it, clear app storage, uninstall the app, or lose access to the device
- if you use Google Drive backup, the backup file remains in your Google Drive app data area until it is replaced, removed through your Google account tools, or otherwise deleted

Because Google Drive backup is stored in the signed-in user's own Google Drive space, users may need to manage removal of that backup through Google account or Google Drive controls if direct in-app deletion is not provided.

## 14. Security

We aim to use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the current architecture of the app. However:

- no software or device environment is completely secure
- locally stored data may be exposed if a device is lost, shared, rooted, jailbroken, infected, or left unlocked
- backups stored in third-party services depend in part on the security of the user's own account and device

Users are responsible for protecting their devices with suitable passwords, screen locks, and account security, especially when the app contains ministry, class, attendance, or child-related information.

## 15. Children and ministry use

StudyMate is intended for Christian education and ministry use across a broad range of ages, including children, youth, adults, teachers, and ministry leaders.

Where adults use the app to manage learner or class information, they should do so consistently with:

- applicable law
- church policy
- school or ministry policy
- parental or guardian expectations

The current mobile release does not require public account creation for normal local study use. Google account sign-in is only tied to optional backup features.

## 16. International use and third-party processing

StudyMate may be used in different countries or regions. Because the app is primarily local-first, most information remains on the user's own device during ordinary use.

If you choose Google Drive backup or use other external services, your information may be processed by service providers such as Google in countries where they operate their infrastructure. Those providers operate under their own terms and privacy commitments.

## 17. Your choices

Depending on how you use the app, you may be able to:

- choose what information to enter into the app
- use the app without enabling Google Drive backup
- sign in with Google only when you want backup features
- disable reminders
- delete notes, bookmarks, highlights, journal entries, roster entries, or other saved data in the app
- clear app data from device settings
- uninstall the app
- revoke Google account access or manage Google Drive app data through your Google account tools
